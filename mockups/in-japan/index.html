    After you reserve

    Japan to our shop in Mesa takes 60 to 90 days. Most of that is waiting for a ship.

    1. Reserve it

      Leave a $1,000 deposit. We check with the exporter that the truck is still for sale, usually within one business day. If it’s gone, you get the deposit back.

    2. Pay the balance

      Due once the exporter confirms. We buy the truck and book it on the next ship with space.

    3. It sails

      Two to eight weeks waiting for a vessel, then three to five weeks at sea to Long Beach.

    4. Customs

      We file the ISF and the customs entry and pay the duty. That cost is already in your price.

    5. Trucked to Mesa

      It comes to our shop by carrier. Arizona title paperwork is prepared before it gets here.

    6. Pick it up

      By appointment at 505 W 8th Ave, Suite 7. The truck is yours as it arrived.

    Questions about buying a truck that’s still in Japan

    Why is this more than the price on Japanese export sites?

    Those sites show the price of the truck in Japan, or the price to a US port. Getting it from there to your driveway adds ocean freight, a 25% import duty on trucks, customs filings, port fees and about $700 of trucking from Long Beach. A $3,850 truck is roughly $8,500 by the time it reaches Mesa. We show that number first, and the breakdown on every listing.

    What does as-is mean here?

    It means the truck is yours in whatever condition it arrives. We have not inspected it, driven it or met the seller. If it shows up with rust or a tired engine, that is the trade for the lower price. If that worries you, use Import by Request, where we choose the truck and guarantee no structural rust and no engine or drivetrain issues.

    How much is the duty?

    Kei trucks pay 25% of the truck’s price in Japan. Kei vans with rear seats are usually entered as passenger vehicles at 2.5%. Each listing shows the duty for that vehicle as its own line.

    Will it come with an Arizona title?

    We prepare the Arizona title paperwork before the truck arrives, the same way we do for Import by Request, so you can register it once you pick it up.

    Are these trucks legal to drive?

    Every truck listed here is at least 25 years old, which makes it legal to import. Road use depends on your state. Our Arizona kei truck legal guide covers the rules here.

    What if the truck sells before you reach the exporter?

    It happens. Listings are checked against the exporter’s stock every morning, but trucks sell during the day. Your deposit is refunded in full, or moved to another truck if you’d rather.
